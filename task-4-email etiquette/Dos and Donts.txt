Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist:


Do’s:


-Think before you post
-Use polite and respectful language
-Keep your personal information private
-Check facts before sharing
-Use privacy settings properly
-Give credit when sharing others’ content
-Report harmful or fake content
-Be kind – spread positivity


Don’ts:


-Don’t post when angry or emotional
-Don’t share your location, phone number, or address
-Don’t bully, insult, or argue rudely
-Don’t believe or spread unverified news
-Don’t post photos of others without permission
-Don’t click on suspicious links
-Don’t use ALL CAPS (it looks like shouting)
-Don’t accept friend requests from strangers
