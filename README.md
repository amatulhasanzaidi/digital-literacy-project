Digital Literacy Awareness Project
Deadline: 31 March 2026,
Objective
The main purpose of this project is to create awareness about Digital Literacy among college students and help them develop essential skills for safe, responsible, and professional use of digital platforms. In today’s world, being digitally literate is as important as academic knowledge. This project combines awareness with practical hands-on activities.
Key Deliverables:
1. Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic Create a visually appealing, informative infographic using Canva that explains the meaning and importance of Digital Literacy.
2. Student Digital Portfolio Design a professional digital portfolio on Canva showcasing personal details, academic background, skills, projects, and achievements.
3. Exploration of Coding and Collaboration Platforms Study and prepare a short report on GitHub, LinkedIn, and Stack Overfl ow, explaining how these platforms are useful for students.
4. Professional Email and Etiquette Guide Prepare ready-to-use professional email templates along with a guide on proper digital etiquette.
5. Cybercrime Awareness Case Study and Prevention Guide.
Final Output:
All the above deliverables will be compiled into one well-designed, single PDF document.
Learning Outcomes:
This project will help students improve their digital skills, understand online safety, learn professional communication, and become responsible digital citizens.
